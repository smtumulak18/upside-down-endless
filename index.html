ABOUT

UPSIDE DOWN: ENDLESS
An endless falling-block arcade game.

Clear lines to level up. Every level is faster: level 2 is twice as fast as level 1, level 5 is five times as fast, and it never stops.

Built with Phaser 3 and Vite. Prototype version.
An unofficial fan-made project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the creators of the TV series that inspired it.
